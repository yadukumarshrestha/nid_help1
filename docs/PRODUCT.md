# NID Help Nepal — Product brief

## Product concept

Build a citizen-first NID navigator, not another government-portal clone or long article directory. The homepage asks what the visitor needs to do and routes them to a short, task-specific guide.

## Core journeys

1. First-time enrolment: eligibility, documents, official online application, assigned office and biometrics.
2. Application status: link directly to the Citizen Portal; never ask for an application/NID number here.
3. eNID: explain where to find the download service, then hand off to the official portal.
4. Replacement: explain the office route and show the current official fee with a verification caveat.
5. Correction: explain where to submit a request and which evidence to prepare.
6. Documents: interactive checklist and print action; no local storage.
7. FAQ and support: searchable common questions, official contact route and source links.

## Product and visual principles

- Task-led, short-answer-first information architecture.
- Strong contrast, large touch targets and responsive layouts.
- Nepali-first, complete English mirror.
- Visible independent-site disclaimer and source context.
- No login, database, analytics storage or user profile.

## Privacy and anti-phishing rules

Never render fields for citizenship number, NID number, application ID, QR receipt, OTP, password, photo, signature, fingerprint, iris or payment data. Never claim to check government status. All sensitive actions must open an official government domain in a new tab with rel=noopener noreferrer.
